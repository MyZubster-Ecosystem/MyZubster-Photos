# MyZubster Photos

Canonical public photo and image repository for the MyZubster ecosystem.

## Structure

- `photos/plants/` — plants, trees and biodiversity
- `photos/gardens/` — gardens and growing spaces
- `photos/urban/` — public urban observations
- `photos/nature/` — environmental and natural observations
- `photos/heritage/` — public cultural and historical subjects
- `photos/robots/` — MyZubster robotics and hardware
- `photos/observations/` — general observation evidence
- `photos/bounties/` — public bounty evidence approved for publication
- `processed/thumbnails/` — generated thumbnails
- `processed/web/` — web-optimized copies
- `metadata/` — public machine-readable metadata
- `docs/` — publishing and privacy policy

## Privacy

Only material explicitly suitable for public publication belongs here.

Do not publish:

- private user identifiers
- JWTs, tokens or credentials
- sensitive locations
- private review evidence
- confidential infrastructure
- unreviewed user uploads

Image metadata such as EXIF/GPS must be reviewed before publication.

## MyZubster

Photos may be referenced by MyZubster applications and public IPFS snapshots.

Publication of an image does not by itself prove bounty verification, reward, payment or settlement.
